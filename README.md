# ⚖️ Legal Solutions

**Legal Solutions** is a responsive, modern legal service landing page designed and developed with a focus on **pixel-perfect implementation** from Figma designs.

---

## 🖌️ Design & Implementation

- Designed and refined in **Figma** with custom edits for better user experience.
- Converted Figma designs into **pixel-perfect front-end code**.
- Implemented using **HTML**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **DaisyUI** for styling and components.
- Fully responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
- Font integration: **EB Garamond** (headings) and **Poppins** (body text).

---

## ⚡ Features

- **Navigation bar** with dropdown and responsive menu for mobile devices.
- **Hero banner** with overlay, search input, and call-to-action button.
- **Service cards** showcasing legal practice areas (Business Law, Criminal Law, Child Support, etc.).
- **Client testimonials carousel** with indicators and navigation buttons.
- **Accordion section** for Frequently Asked Questions.
- **Contact section** with information cards and a functional contact form.
- **Footer** with subscription form, contact info, and quick links.
- Custom **color theme** and branding consistent with Figma design.
